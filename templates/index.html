This dashboard pulls the last 7 days of real-time locational marginal price (LMP) data straight from the public historical reports of two grid operators, ISO-NE (New England) and NYISO (New York), and streams it to the browser as it's fetched. Chart up to 4 sites at once per ISO, compare them on a combined chart, and see the week's biggest price events in one table.

Glossary

LMP
Locational Marginal Price: the real-time cost of energy at a specific grid location, in $/MWh.
Site ID / Location ID / PTID
The identifier for a pricing node on the grid. ISO-NE calls it a Location ID; NYISO calls it a PTID.
ISO
Independent System Operator: the regional grid operator that publishes this pricing data.
